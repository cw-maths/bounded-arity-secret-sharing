1. THE FIXED TABLE AND PARAMETERS

P is the finite type of physical participants and N = |P|. Gamma is the
approved proper monotone access structure. The secret is the distinct index
none in Option P, and participant p has index some p. Let r >= 2 and

    c_r = choose(r - 2, floor((r - 2)/2)).

The witness is the real-valued version of the existing natural-valued table:

    F(X) = (formalTable Gamma (arityConstant r) X : Real).

This choice depends on P, Gamma and r alone. It is made before any field,
inequality coefficients, grouping, or local representation is selected.
All earlier definitions, including this formal table, are unchanged.

2. E31 — EXACT FEASIBILITY DOMAIN

FeasibleAtArity r Gamma F is the conjunction of:

  (a) IsPolymatroid F: F(empty)=0, monotonicity and submodularity on ALL
      subsets of Option P.
  (b) HasNormalizedAccess Gamma F: F({none})=1 and every reviewed privacy /
      reconstruction equality.
  (c) For EVERY natural k <= r, EVERY real coefficient family a indexed by
      subsets of Fin k, and EVERY grouping A : Fin k -> Finset (Option P),
      if a is a valid homogeneous information inequality, then

          sum over I subset Fin k of a(I) * F(union_{j in I} A(j)) >= 0.

There is one F outside all of these quantifiers. k=0 and k=1 are included.
Groups may be empty, repeated, overlapping, arbitrarily large, and may include
the secret. Coefficients are arbitrary reals, with no additive constant and
no restriction to a selected list of inequalities.

ValidInformation retains the E28/E29 domain: arbitrary countable alphabets,
one common joint law, and finite full-joint entropy. See E28_comparison.txt.

3. PRESCRIBED-FIELD RANK CONSTRAINTS

RankConstraintsAtArity K r F repeats the same universal grouping/arity
condition using ValidRank K. K has only the Field assumption: it may be finite
or infinite, in any characteristic and of any size. RankFeasibleAtArity K r
Gamma F conjoins the entire information-feasibility condition with these rank
constraints. Both apply to the SAME table.

barrier_checkpoint fixes the explicit F above and proves simultaneously:

    FeasibleAtArity r Gamma F
    AND, for every field K, RankConstraintsAtArity K r F
    AND, for every physical participant p,
        F({some p}) = 1 + c_r*(N - 1)
    AND MaxShareAtMost F (1 + c_r*(N - 1)).

barrier_exists_witness chooses F before the universal quantifier over fields.
Both statements also have the quantifiers over inequalities and groupings
inside their feasibility predicates, as required by T8.

4. E32 — THE ACTUAL MAXIMUM OBJECTIVE

The new convenience definition maxShare F is the finite supremum of
F({some p}) over physical participants p. Properness of Gamma is proved to
make P nonempty; maxShare_attained proves this supremum is attained and hence
is an actual maximum. maxShareAtMost_iff proves exactly

    MaxShareAtMost F L <-> maxShare F <= L.

This relates the explicit maximum to the pointwise objective definition
already approved in E32. Secret normalization is supplied by item 2(b), so
these are normalized share values. The witness has EXACT maximum

    maxShare F = 1 + c_r*(N - 1).

5. THE TWO INFIMUM CEILINGS

FeasibleShareValues r Gamma is the set of values maxShare F attained by
FeasibleAtArity r Gamma tables. relaxationValue r Gamma is its infimum.
The corresponding rank-strengthened definitions use RankFeasibleAtArity K.
The code proves these sets are nonempty for r>=2 and bounded below by zero.
Therefore the infimum theorems have their intended mathematical meaning:

    relaxationValue r Gamma <= 1 + c_r*(N - 1),

    rankRelaxationValue K r Gamma <= 1 + c_r*(N - 1)
        for every prescribed field K.

The additional theorem relaxationValue_le_rankRelaxationValue proves that
adjoining rank constraints can only increase the objective infimum.
No assertion is made that the ceiling is the optimum for every Gamma.

6. SIX-VARIABLE SPECIALIZATION

arityConstant 6 = 6. Properness gives N>=1, so the natural-number formula
casts to the ordinary real expression 6*N - 5. Both relaxation_ceiling_six
and rank_relaxation_ceiling_six have that exact displayed bound.

UNDERSTANDING — OPTIONAL PROOF OUTLINE

Pad a k-group query with empty groups until it has r groups. Apply the exact
T6 representation over the requested field, then restrict that single model
to the original labels. Over the binary field T7 supplies one finite joint
entropy law with exactly the same entries. Apply each universally valid
inequality to its model. T3 already gives the whole-table Shannon/access
constraints and the singleton formula. The fixed table is therefore feasible
and attains the stated maximum, placing that value inside each objective set;
the infimum is at most this attained value.
