SPECIFICATION CORRESPONDENCE

1. Arbitrary countable alphabets.
   DiscreteJointLaw contains Alphabet : G -> Type v, together with a proof
   Countable (Alphabet j) for every j. The alphabets are data chosen with
   the law; no particular alphabet is fixed. Label and alphabet universes
   are independent. IsDiscreteEntropyTable has no finite-alphabet condition.

2. One joint probability mass function.
   The pmf field is PMF ((j : G) -> Alphabet j). A PMF includes a proof that
   its nonnegative masses sum to one. The existential quantifier over this
   one law precedes the universal quantifier over all coalitions.

3. Marginals of that same law.
   DiscreteJointLaw.marginal I is the pushforward of law.pmf by coordinate
   restriction to I. There is no independently chosen law for each I.

4. Base-two Shannon entropy, with 0 log 0 = 0.
   pmfEntropyTerm is the extended nonnegative real value of
   Real.negMulLog(p.toReal) / Real.log 2. Real.negMulLog is -p log p.
   The zero summand is proved to be zero. pmf_entropy_summand_nonneg proves
   genuine PMF summands are nonnegative, so ENNReal.ofReal clips nothing.
   pmfEntropy is the extended sum of these contributions, possibly infinity.

5. Finiteness of full joint entropy.
   law.FiniteJointEntropy is exactly pmfEntropy law.pmf != infinity.
   finiteJointEntropy_iff_univ proves equivalence with finiteness of the
   full marginal. It is not a finite-support or finite-alphabet condition.

6. Real table entries are justified by that finiteness condition.
   pmfEntropy_map_le proves deterministic observation cannot increase entropy,
   including infinite entropy. jointEntropyENN_ne_top deduces finiteness for
   every marginal from finite full-joint entropy. jointEntropy_hasSum proves
   the real entry equals the convergent Shannon series. Lean's real conversion
   is a total function, but IsDiscreteEntropyTable explicitly requires finite
   full-joint entropy and these theorems justify every use as real entropy.

7. Empty-tuple entropy.
   jointEntropy_empty and IsDiscreteEntropyTable.empty prove the empty entry
   is zero. It follows from the unique empty tuple, not an additional axiom.

8. Finite witnesses within the approved broader domain.
   IsFiniteEntropyTable adds finite alphabets to the same one-law contract.
   IsFiniteEntropyTable.isDiscreteEntropyTable proves such witnesses belong
   to the full approved countable-alphabet domain. T7 constructs finite
   witnesses; ValidInformation continues to quantify over the broader domain.

T7 STATEMENT AND PROVED DEPENDENCIES

rank_entropy_checkpoint constructs one finite joint law whose entries are
    H(Z_I) = g(I) * (log |K| / log 2)
for all coalitions I. The proof uses the uniform law on the dual of the
ambient coordinate space; each label observes the functional restricted to
its subspace. The kernel is the annihilator of the sum of those subspaces,
so the joint observation rank is g(I). Linear fibers have equal size, the
image has |K|^g(I) elements, and its uniform entropy is g(I)*log_2|K|.
These probability, fiber-count, dimension, and entropy facts are proved.

finiteField_entropyScale_pos proves log_2|K| > 0. Homogeneous scaling is
proved in InformationInequalities.lean and used by information_rank_checkpoint.
Over F_2 the common factor is exactly 1; binary_rank_entropy_checkpoint and
localEntropy_checkpoint give exact entropy tables in bits for T6 projections.
The grouping is fixed before its common witness law is chosen. Different
groupings may have different witness laws.
